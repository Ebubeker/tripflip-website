About TripFlip

TripFlip was born from a simple idea: travel planning should be exciting, not exhausting. Founded in 2025 by a solo-team of travel enthusiasts and AI experts, we set out to revolutionize how people discover and plan their perfect trips.

Our mission is to make travel accessible to everyone by leveraging cutting-edge artificial intelligence to provide personalized recommendations that match your unique style, budget, and preferences. We believe that every journey should be memorable, and every traveler deserves the perfect trip.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted AI-powered travel companion, making every journey extraordinary.
